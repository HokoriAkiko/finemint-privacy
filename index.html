Privacy Policy

Your finances are personal. Your privacy should be too.

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

FineMint is a personal finance tracking application designed to help you manage your income, expenses, transactions, and monthly category limits. We value your privacy and aim to keep your financial information under your control.

1. Information We Store

FineMint allows you to enter and manage information related to your personal finances, including:

  • Income and expense amounts.
  • Transaction descriptions and dates.
  • Transaction categories.
  • Monthly category limits.
  • Application preferences and settings.

This information is stored locally on your device. FineMint does not require you to create an account or provide personal details to use its core features.

2. Data Collection and Sharing

FineMint does not operate its own server to collect or store your personal financial records.

We do not sell, rent, or share your financial information with third parties.

Your financial entries are not transmitted to FineMint servers for storage or processing.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your financial information remains on your device. Its security also depends on the security measures enabled on your device, such as screen locks and device encryption.

FineMint does not currently provide cloud synchronization or an online account-based backup service.

4. Notifications

FineMint may use local notifications to remind you to record transactions or engage with the application.

These notifications are scheduled on your device. You can manage or disable notification permissions through your Android device settings.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your financial information remains stored on your device until you delete it within the application, clear the application's storage, or uninstall FineMint.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its storage may permanently delete locally stored financial records. We cannot recover information that has been deleted from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

FineMint is a personal finance utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

FineMint does not operate a user registration or personal financial data collection system.

7. Third-Party Services

FineMint does not integrate third-party advertising services or analytics tools for collecting your personal financial records.

If third-party services are introduced in future versions, this policy will be updated to explain any relevant changes to data handling.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when FineMint's features or privacy practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page along with its revised effective date.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.
