FineMint Privacy Policy Effective date: November 25, 2026 1. Introduction FineMint is a personal finance tracking application designed to help users manage their income, expenses, transactions, and monthly category limits. Your privacy is important to us. FineMint is designed to store your financial records locally on your device, without requiring an account or transmitting your financial information to our servers. 2. Information Stored FineMint may store information you voluntarily enter into the application, including: - Income and expense amounts - Transaction descriptions - Transaction dates - Categories - Monthly category limits - App preferences and settings This information is stored locally on your device. 3. Data Collection and Sharing FineMint does not operate its own server to collect or store your financial information. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal financial records with third parties. FineMint does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its core features. 4. Data Storage and Security Your financial records are stored locally on your device. Their security depends partly on your device's security measures, such as screen lock and device encryption. FineMint does not currently provide cloud synchronization or an online account-based backup system. 5. Notifications FineMint may use local notifications to remind you to record transactions or engage with the application. Notifications are scheduled on your device. You can control notification permissions through your Android device settings. 6. Data Retention and Deletion Your financial information remains stored on your device until you delete it within the application, clear the application's storage, or uninstall FineMint. Uninstalling the application or clearing its storage may permanently remove locally stored financial records. 7. Children's Privacy FineMint is a personal finance utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. It does not operate a user registration or data collection system. 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when FineMint's features or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be made available through the privacy policy webpage. 9.
